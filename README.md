## 💡 Our Philosophy

Weather apps should be clean, fast, and respectful of your privacy. **Nimbus Weather** contains **zero ads, zero invasive tracking, and no subscriptions.**

The core version of Nimbus Weather is – and will always remain – **100% free**. If you want to support independent development, you can unlock **Nimbus Weather Pro** for a fair, one-time purchase that stays on your Google Account forever!

## ✨ Features Breakdown

### 🆓 Free Edition (Always Free)
- **Privacy-First:** Location data is used strictly to fetch forecasts and radar. Zero tracking, zero ads.
- **Complete Weather Dashboard:** Current conditions, feels-like temperature, high/low, UV index, barometer, wind, gusts, humidity, pressure, cloud cover, dew point, and precipitation.
- **24-Hour & 14-Day Forecasts:** Complete hourly overview for tomorrow and a full 14-day outlook.
- **Multi-Location Support:** Save and seamlessly swap between up to 10 places directly from the header.
- **Dynamic Sky Backgrounds:** Background animations match the real weather outside.
- **3 Free Home-Screen Widgets:** 
  - `2×2 Dashboard` (Now, feels-like, high/low)
  - `4×1 Forecast Bar` (Now and next 4 hours)
  - `4×2 Daily Outlook` (Now and next 4 days)

---

### 🚀 Nimbus Weather Pro Features (One-Time Purchase)
- **📱 All 9 Home-Screen Widgets & Widget Studio:** Unlocks 6 additional layouts (`2×1 Minimal`, `2×2 Daily`, `2×2 Metrics`, `2×2 Sunset`, `4×2 Hourly`, and `4×4 Master`). Fully customize metrics, transparency, text size, severe-weather marks, and quick tap actions.
- **🧠 Weather Intelligence:** Smart breakdowns of rain windows, storm risks, pressure trends, and the best hour to be outside.
- **🤖 Radar AI & Time Machine:** AI-analyzed RainViewer radar playback across a 2-hour history (10-min steps). Scrub through today hour-by-hour with detailed metrics.
- **🌿 Seasons & Phenology Tracker:** Automatically tracks first frost, first snow, leaf fall, blossom starts, heat days (>30 °C), and tropical nights (≥20 °C) — inverted for the Southern Hemisphere.
- **🩺 Biometeorology & Health Tracker:** Comprehensive pollen tracking (grass, birch, alder, ragweed), air quality index (PM2.5, PM10), and specialized notes for migraine, asthma, and rheumatism.
- **🔔 Smart Alerts & Special Effects:** Severe weather warnings, morning/evening briefings, exclusive themes (*Dark Glass* & *Atmosphere*), wind compass, and rain-on-glass visuals.
